Good to Know

What counts as a "revision round"?

One revision round = one batch of change requests submitted together after reviewing a completed design or build stage. Individual edits within that batch don't count separately. Revisions submitted piecemeal over multiple days count as separate rounds.

When do I pay?

Payment is made upfront through secure Stripe checkout when you choose a plan. Click a plan on the pricing page, complete checkout, and we’ll get started. Build packages are a one-time payment; hosting plans are billed monthly through the same checkout. Have questions first, or need something custom? Send us a message and we’ll sort it out before you pay.

What if I need to cancel mid-project?

If you cancel mid-project, you’ll receive a partial refund of any prepaid amount for work we haven’t yet performed. Work completed up to the date of cancellation is non-refundable.

Do I have to host with you?

No. You have the option to host and maintain your site with us through one of the plans above, but it’s not required. If you’d rather take the build and host it yourself, we can hand off the project at launch — just let us know up front so we can scope it accordingly.

Do you offer rush delivery?

Yes. Rush fee is 25% of the package price for timelines under the standard window.
