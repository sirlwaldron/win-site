Edits, New Work, and Add-ons

Edit hours

  • Plan hours are used month by month. Unused hours don’t roll over.
  • Hours beyond your plan, and edits on a no-edit plan, are billed at $50/hr. We quote anything over an hour before we start.

What counts as an edit (uses your plan hours)

  • Changing text, swapping images, updating hours, prices, staff bios, and contact details
  • Small layout tweaks on existing pages
  • Fixing broken links or small display problems that come from a content change
  • Adding or removing a simple item on an existing page (e.g. a service listing)

What counts as new work (quoted separately at $50/hr or a flat price)

  • New pages, new sections, or redesigned layouts
  • New features, forms, integrations, or checkout changes
  • Blog setup or ongoing content writing
  • Logo and brand identity work, photography, ad campaign management
  • Anything that wasn’t in your original package

Bug fixes vs. edits. Something we built that doesn’t work as described is a bug. Bugs in the post-launch window (30 days on Starter, 60 on Growth and Systems Build) are fixed free. After that, bug fixes may be quoted separately.

Third-party paid add-ons are billed at cost to you with no markup, and we ask first. This covers premium plugins and paid integration plans (e.g. Zapier), stock images beyond the free libraries, paid fonts, Google Workspace, and domain types beyond standard .com/.net/.org.

Rush delivery: 25% of the package price for timelines under the standard window.

Cancellation, Handoff & Ownership

  • Cancel a hosting plan: 30 days’ written notice. Billing continues through the notice period, and amounts billed during the notice period aren’t refunded.

Good to Know

What counts as a “revision round”?

One revision round is one batch of change requests submitted together after reviewing a completed design or build stage. Individual edits within that batch don’t count separately. Revisions sent piecemeal over multiple days count as separate rounds.

When do I pay?

Upfront through secure Stripe checkout. Pick your plan in the pricing section above and click Get Started (builds) or Subscribe (hosting plans). Build packages are a one-time payment. Hosting plans bill monthly through the same checkout. Not sure which one fits? Message us first and we’ll sort it out before you pay.

Do I have to host with you?

No. If you’d rather host it yourself, we hand off the project at launch. Let us know up front so we can scope it. You’ll own your hosting and domain accounts and pay for them directly.

Who pays for the domain?

If you host with us, domain renewal is included in your plan while it is active, and the domain is registered in your name. Renewal, DNS, and SSL stop when your plan ends or goes unpaid. If you host yourself, you pay your registrar directly. Either way, you own the domain.

Is business email included?

No. Google Workspace is optional. We set it up (a one-time setup fee, quoted before we start), and Google bills you directly, at about $7 per user per month (annual) or $8.40 per user per month (month-to-month).

Do my unused edit hours carry over?

No. Hours reset each month. Extra work is $50/hr.

Who pays Stripe’s fees on my online store?

You do. Stripe takes its processing fee from each sale on your own Stripe account.

What if I need to cancel mid-project?

You’ll receive a partial refund of any prepaid amount for work we haven’t yet performed. Work completed up to the date of cancellation is non-refundable.

What if I cancel hosting?

Send 30 days’ written notice. Billing continues through the notice period, and amounts billed during the notice period aren’t refunded. We then hand off your site files and point your domain wherever you want it, at no charge. Domain renewal, DNS, and SSL stop when your plan ends, so after handoff you renew the domain with your registrar.

Do you offer rush delivery?

Yes. Rush fee is 25% of the package price for timelines under the standard window.

What’s not included in any package?

Logo design or brand identity work, professional photography or stock image licensing beyond basic use, ongoing content creation or blog posts, paid ad campaign management. These are available as add-ons. Just ask.
