Urgent calls and messages

  • Calls you mark as urgent are transferred to a number you choose, such as your cell or an on-call line.
  • Everything else becomes a message: caller name, number, and what they need, sent to you by text and email.
  • If nobody picks up a transfer, the caller is offered a message instead of being dropped.

Recording and privacy

  • Callers are told at the start of the call that they are speaking with an AI assistant.
  • Call recordings and transcripts are optional. You decide whether they are kept.
  • Call recording laws vary by state. We help you set the right notice, and you stay in control of your settings.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It covers the calls nobody can pick up and the routine questions that interrupt your day. Your team stays focused on the work that needs a person.

What if it does not know the answer?

It only answers from the information you give us. For anything outside that, it does not guess. It takes a message and sends it to you by text and email so you can follow up.

Will callers know it is an AI?

Yes. Every call opens by letting the caller know they are speaking with an AI assistant, and callers can ask to leave a message for a person.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Calls forward from your existing business number. You can turn forwarding on or off with your phone carrier whenever you like.

Can it work alongside my website?

Yes. We design and build websites, so your site, your contact form, and your receptionist can point customers to the same booking path.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. You can stay month-to-month and pay the one-time setup fee, or commit to 3 or more months and we waive the setup fee.

How do I get started?

Book a 30-minute call or send us a message. We will talk through how your calls work today and whether an AI receptionist fits. No obligation.
